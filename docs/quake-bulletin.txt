Kestrel Basin Seismic Authority - Event Bulletin
Issued: 10 September 2026

A magnitude 6.4 earthquake struck the Kestrel Basin on 10 September 2026 at 04:12
local time. The epicentre was 12 km north of Halden Ridge at a depth of 8 km.
Structural damage has been reported across three districts. This bulletin confirms
the event exceeded magnitude 6.0 and originated within the Kestrel Basin.
