GenLayer Asimov

Insurance that pays when a public source shows the peril happened in time.

An underwriter names a peril, the page where its truth will show, a payout, a premium and a window. Someone takes the policy and pays the premium. After that, anyone can settle it: the contract fetches the page, tells the round what the window was, and a round of GenLayer validators decides whether the peril occurred on or before the window closed. If it did, the payout moves to the insured. No claim, no adjuster. If the window closes untriggered, the policy expires.
